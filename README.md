# Everything BIM
### An Android application that'll make navigating Barbados more convenient by providing tourists and locals with a digital guide companion in their pocket.

The country of Barbados is a popular tourist destination, but with such a small Island, formulaic itineraries - “Go this place this day” and “visit this location at this time” you can feel like you’ve seen everything in one visit when you haven’t. This is not conducive to repeat visits, and what we seek to accomplish with our mobile application is to make all of Barbados more accessible.

### What does it do?
- Provide location identification using an AI model trained on Barbados landmarks.
- Offer GPS navigation via Google Maps API.
- Display nearby hotspots filtered by type, distance, and rating.
- Allow user-generated content: posts, reviews, ratings, comments.
- Enable business accounts to register, verify, and manage their business profiles.
